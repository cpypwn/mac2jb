# Safety notice

**Use Super File Bros. with caution and at your own risk.** Modifying, corrupting or deleting console or SD-card files can have unexpected, non-obvious consequences, including problems that appear later. You may lose screenshots, saves, installed content or access to applications. A file that seems unimportant may be required by other software.

Back up important data before making changes. Permanent deletion cannot be undone by this application. Deleting a folder includes all its contents. Interrupted writes, network failures, competing tools and console crashes may leave incomplete data; transfer completion is not a guarantee that a file is suitable for its intended use. Do not edit files you do not understand, particularly installed-content folders.

SD management controls do not make changes inherently safe. Internal console mounts and FTP exports remain read-only in this version. Use a trusted computer and local network, do not expose FTP to the Internet, and finish operations before closing the original launcher.
